How it works

Direct N-body with Plummer softening and a leapfrog integrator. The force sum runs on the GPU. Initial conditions follow the paper: P1 is a King W0 = 7 model with rh = 5 pc, P2 is the same model shrunk, the masses come from a Kroupa IMF over 0.1–50 M☉, and Z = 0.0001.

A few thousand particles stand in for 40 000 stars. Time is scaled by the half-mass relaxation time, so relaxation and stellar evolution keep their real ratio. The Galactic tide is a circular orbit in a flat 220 km/s rotation curve, scaled to keep rt/rh. Neutron stars always leave on natal kicks.
